Per Ankh privacy policy

Effective 21 October 2026. Per Ankh is published by LOVI Tech.

In short

Per Ankh works offline and collects nothing about you. The only data that ever leaves your phone is a problem report that you write yourself, in tester mode, and choose to send. Reports carry no name, email address, account or device identifier.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (what you have learned, your reviews, awards) and your settings are stored only on your phone. If Android's backup is on, Android may copy them to your own Google account, under Google's terms; we never receive that copy. Deleting the app deletes the data.

Problem reports (tester mode)

Tester mode is off unless you turn it on in Settings. With it on, a flag on lesson pages lets you write a report. Reports are saved on your phone and are sent only when you tap Send reports in Settings. A report contains:

  • the category you pick and the note you type (please do not put personal details in it);
  • what was on screen: the lesson, the exercise, the hieroglyphs, transliterations and options shown, the answer you gave and how it was marked, and which sound clip played;
  • the app version and the Android version number.

It does not contain your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, any account, advertising ID or device ID.

Reports travel over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) directly to our server at perankh.hrana.app, hosted by Akamai Cloud (Linode). No other company sees their contents. The server keeps no record of where a report came from: it writes no access log, and holds your network address in memory only to limit how often reports can be sent. Server error logs, kept for up to 14 days for security, may contain network addresses.

We use reports only to find and fix problems in the course. We never sell them, share them, use them for advertising, or combine them with other data.

How long we keep reports

A report is kept while the problem is open, and deleted within 12 months of being closed. Because reports are anonymous we cannot find “your” reports; if you want one deleted, tell us roughly when you sent it and what it said, and we will remove it.

Children

Per Ankh is a general-audience learning app and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If a future version adds anything that collects more data, such as optional accounts to sync progress between devices, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and that feature will be optional. Questions and requests: open an issue at github.com/ekoori/PerAnkh/issues.
